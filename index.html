Personal Gift Share

If a host receives 100,000 Coins during a broadcast, the host receives 100,000 Red Diamonds.

Example:
100,000 Coins received × 100% gift share = 100,000 Red Diamonds

Weekly Room Bonus

Hosts earn 60% of the Room Bonus, based on the room’s total income. The Room Bonus is manually credited once every week, every Monday.

Example:
100,000 Red Diamonds (Room Target) x 60% = 60,000 Red Diamonds

Weekly Time Bonus

Complete 4 active days and 10 broadcast hours in a week to qualify for the 25% Weekly Time Bonus.

Example:
Weekly Room Bonus: 60,000 Red Diamonds
Time Bonus: 60,000 × 25% = 15,000 Red Diamonds

# IMPORTANT POINTS

Key conversion and cashout rates.

1. Salary Conversion Rate

25,000 Red Diamonds = $1 USD

The salary calculation rate.

2. Host Cashout Rate

25,000 Red Diamonds = $1 USD = ₹95 INR

Hosts can cash out their Red Diamonds through a Merchant.
Example: If a host has 250,000 Red Diamonds, the value is: 250,000 ÷ 25,000 = $10 = ₹950
